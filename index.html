Frequently Asked Questions

How is the coaching structured?
Every coaching session is anchored to established management canons, including the SBI framework, Crucial Conversations structure, and psychological safety research, tailored specifically to your upcoming calendar events.

What data does it read from Slack/Calendar?
It reads meeting titles and participant graphs to trigger prep briefs at the right time. Personal data is redacted before hitting the model, and we operate on zero-data-retention (ZDR) endpoints so your data is never used to train the AI.
