Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pexera?
Pexera is a platform for meaningful professional 1:1 meetings. It helps professionals find peers for short virtual coffees to get acquainted and share insights.

Is this just casual chatting?
The meetings have a clear professional context. They help participants find specialized knowledge, get career advice, or synchronize on industry trends.

Who is this platform for?
It's for ambitious individuals, networking groups, and organizations looking to expand their circle of contacts, break down silos, and foster organic knowledge exchange.

How does matching work?
Based on indicated professional interests and roles. The system intelligently pairs you with someone who aligns with your specific conversation goals.

What about privacy & security?
We prioritize a spam-free environment. All connections and conversations are strictly opt-in, maintaining a high standard for authentic professional exchange.

What analytics are provided?
We offer aggregated data insights: overall network activity, trending topics, and match success rates. Private messages remain completely confidential.
