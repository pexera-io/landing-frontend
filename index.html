Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pexera?
Pexera is a B2B platform that curates strategic, cross-company 1:1 introductions. It is designed to help revenue, partnerships, and R&D teams expand their network with highly relevant peers, automatically.

Is this a networking app?
It is an outcome-driven introduction system. Unlike open networking platforms, Pexera acts as a closed, managed environment where introductions are deliberately curated based on exact business objectives, not random browsing.

Who is Pexera for?
Pexera is built for professionals where a strong external network directly impacts their goals. This includes Business Development, Strategic Partnerships, Sales Leaders, Product Managers, R&D, and Executive Leadership.

Is matching random?
Absolutely not. Our algorithm only generates a match when there is a clear, overlapping business goal, role alignment, and appropriate seniority level. We respect your time, so if there isn't a high-signal match available immediately, you wait until one is found.

How does the scheduling work?
Currently, users propose and agree on a time directly via the secure internal chat interface upon matching.

Is post-meeting feedback required?
Yes. It is a mandatory step in our structured workflow. This closed-loop system is how we guarantee the continued quality of introductions and track overall network ROI for your company.

Are conversations private?
Yes. Pexera provides a secure matching infrastructure, but all direct messaging and meeting contents remain entirely private between the two matched professionals.

What data does the company Admin see?
Administrators access an aggregated dashboard showing participation rates, successful introduction counts, and high-level trends regarding the business goals their teams are discussing. No private chats or individual feedback details are shared.

How often do matches happen?
Cadence is flexible based on your team's capacity. You can opt-in for introductions as often as your schedule permits, provided there is a high-signal match available.

How do teams gain access?
Pexera operates on an enterprise model. Company administrators set up a secure workspace and issue invites to specific departments or employees. Request a demo to get started.
