Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pexera?
Pexera helps professionals from different companies meet for short 1:1 online coffee conversations. It’s a simple, regular way to talk to new people, exchange experience, and feel more connected outside your own company.

Is this networking?
Yes. We really value classic networking and events. Pexera is simply another format: more one-on-one, more regular, and easier to fit into everyday work.

Is Pexera for social chats after work?
It’s work-focused, but still human. Conversations are professional, relaxed, and natural.

Who is Pexera for?
For people who want to grow their professional circle, learn how others work, and stay connected to the wider industry.

For companies that want to support engagement, development, and healthy external connections.

What roles is Pexera for?
Pexera is role-agnostic. It works for any role your company decides to include: specialists, managers, team leads, founders, HR, sales, marketing, product, tech, operations, or mixed groups. Some companies open it to everyone. Others start with specific teams. Both approaches work.

How does matching work?
You choose to look for a coffee partner. The system matches you with someone based on role, specialization, and professional interests. If no one is available at that moment, the match happens automatically once someone appears.

Do I meet people from my own company?
No. Pexera focuses on cross-company conversations to bring fresh perspectives.

How often can I have coffee chats?
There’s no strict weekly limit in the MVP. After each conversation, you leave feedback and can then go for the next one.

Is matching random?
Mostly interest-based. The system prioritizes shared professional context and interests. Random matching is only used when there are no overlaps.

How do we schedule the call?
For now, people agree on time directly in the chat. Calendar integrations are planned later.

What if the meeting didn’t happen?
Both participants confirm the outcome. If confirmations don’t match, the meeting isn’t counted.

Is feedback required?
Yes. It’s a short step that helps keep the experience thoughtful and meaningful. The feedback is mainly for your own insights and platform learning.

Who can see my feedback?
Only you. Companies see only aggregated, anonymous insights.

Are conversations private?
Yes. Messages are private. Pexera doesn’t read chats or share their content.

What does the company see?
High-level activity and engagement data, such as number of coffees and overall trends. No personal messages or individual notes.

Is Pexera about recruiting or job offers?
No. It’s about professional exchange and connection. Companies can also set clear participation rules if needed.

How do people join?
Through an invite from HR or management. After onboarding, users are ready to match.

Can users change their email themselves?
Email changes go through HR to keep things simple and secure.

What problem does Pexera solve?
It helps people feel less professionally isolated, build stronger networking habits, and grow diverse professional connections.

What results does Pexera track?
Engagement, regularity of conversations, and changes in how connected people feel professionally.

Does Pexera promise sales or instant results?
No promises like that. Pexera focuses on building a healthy, human connection process. Results grow naturally over time.
